# 📊 Regulatory Compliance Management Platform — ReguTrack

## 🚀 Project Overview
A compliance management platform designed for enterprises to efficiently manage and track regulatory requirements such as GDPR, ISO, and SEBI. The system helps organizations align regulations with internal controls, assign responsibilities, monitor progress, and ensure timely compliance.

---

## 🎯 Key Features

### 1. Regulatory Mapping
- Map regulatory requirements (GDPR, ISO, SEBI) to internal company controls
- Centralized view of all compliance obligations

### 2. Task Assignment
- Assign compliance responsibilities to specific teams or individuals
- Track ownership and accountability

### 3. Evidence Tracking
- Upload and manage compliance evidence
- Maintain records for audits and verification

### 4. Audit Scheduling
- Schedule internal and external audits
- Track audit status and findings

### 5. Progress Monitoring
- Real-time compliance status dashboard
- Alert and notification system for upcoming deadlines

### 6. Reporting & Analytics
- Generate compliance reports by regulation, department, or date range
- Export CSV/PDF reports for stakeholders

---

## 🏗️ Tech Stack
- **Frontend**: React (Vite), Vanilla CSS, Recharts
- **Backend**: Node.js, Express.js
- **Database**: MongoDB (Mongoose)
- **Auth**: JWT (JSON Web Tokens)

## 📋 User Roles
- **Admin**: Full access — manage users, rules, audits, reports
- **Compliance Officer**: Manage compliance rules, audits, evidence
- **Viewer**: Read-only access to dashboards and reports
